## From SIP endpoints to the call center

- **PBX & call centers:** Asterisk, Issabel and FreePBX; SIP trunks, IVR and call queues.
- **Operations & integrations:** call recording, CDR reporting and CRM integration.
- **Infrastructure & support:** branch connectivity, on-premises deployment, troubleshooting, backups and maintenance.

`SIP / VoIP` `Kotlin` `C++` `Qt / QML` `Asterisk` `Issabel` `FreePBX` `Linux`
